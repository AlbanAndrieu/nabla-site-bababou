Central Authorities documents (CA)

Access to original documents

Original documents are only available upon request for Lawyers, Institutions and Authorities to access their contents.

If you need access, send an email to alban.andrieu@bababou.com with subject: URGENT, bababou: Acces to paperless NGX requested.

Documents on this website — processing notice

Documents provided on this website have been processed as follows: spelling and grammar fixes, anonymization (to protect the child), and translation when required. Otherwise they remain as close as possible to the original.

Some documents are missing because they require validation before being published.
